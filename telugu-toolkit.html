ABOUT THE APP

Telugu Toolkit: All Utility

Key Features & Tools:
1. Specialized Financial Calculators:
Village Interest Calculator: Calculate monthly interest based on local village customs (e.g., ₹2 or ₹3 per ₹100). Supports both Simple and Compound interest.
Gold & Silver Price: Get real-time gold and silver rates with automatic GST and making charges calculation to know the final price before you buy.
EMI Calculator: Plan your Home, Car, or Personal loans with a detailed monthly breakdown and amortization schedule.
GST Calculator: Instantly calculate GST (Inclusive or Exclusive) for billing and business needs.
SIP & Investment: Plan your future with our Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Percentage calculators.
2. Land & Unit Converters:
Land Converter: Specialized for local measurements! Convert between Acres, Guntas, Cents, Square Yards (Gajalu), and Square Feet effortlessly.
All-in-One Converter: High-speed conversion for Length, Weight (Mass), Temperature, Area, Time, Data, and Volume.
Number System: Convert between Decimal, Binary, Hex, and Octal systems.
3. Essential Daily Utilities:
Vastu Compass: Align your home and workspace with a built-in Vastu-compliant digital compass showing all major directions (Eeshanyam, Agneyam, etc.).
Age Calculator: Calculate your exact age in years, months, and days, and see how many days are left for your next birthday.
Fuel & Mileage: Calculate trip costs based on distance, fuel price, and your vehicle's mileage.
BMI Calculator: Monitor your health with our BMI calculator, complete with health status and ideal weight tips.
Unit Price Finder: Compare two products to find out which one offers the best value for your money.
4. Quick Links & Emergency Services:
State Portals: Direct access to Dharani (TS), Meebhoomi (AP), and Electricity Bill payments (TGSPDCL/APEPDCL).
Traffic E-Challan: Quickly check traffic fines for Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
Emergency Contact List: One-tap access to Police (100), Fire (101), Ambulance (108), Women Helpline (181), and more.
Why Choose Telugu Toolkit?
Fully Localized: Switch between Telugu and English with a single tap.
Beautiful & Modern UI: A clean, professional design that is easy on the eyes and supports Dark Mode.
Privacy First: No unnecessary permissions. Your data and privacy are our top priority.
Frequently Used Tools: The app learns your habits and puts your most-used tools right on the dashboard for quick access.
Completely Offline: Most calculators work 100% offline (except for real-time gold prices and external web portals).
