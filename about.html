OUR STORY

Built for the modern minimalist.

We started Shankarbros.dev with a simple realization: our devices were demanding too much of our attention. Notifications, complex interfaces, and bloatware were getting in the way of actually getting things done.

So we decided to build the tools we wanted to use. Apps that do exactly one thing very well, open instantly, and get out of your way. Seven years later, we are proud to serve a growing community of users who share our philosophy of digital minimalism.
