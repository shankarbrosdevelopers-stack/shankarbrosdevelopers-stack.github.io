ABOUT THE APP

Morse Code Flash: SOS Survival

Morse Code Flash is your ultimate tactical flashlight transmitter, telegraph key simulator, and interactive Morse code trainer. Whether you are signaling an emergency SOS in the wilderness, learning radio telegraphy, or practicing ham radio Q-codes, this all-in-one utility delivers instant, reliable communication.

⚡ OPTICAL FLASHLIGHT & SCREEN TRANSMITTER
• Flashlight Torch Blinker: Automatically translate any text into high-precision optical Morse light pulses using your camera LED torch.
• Emergency SOS Mode: Instant one-tap SOS strobe beacon designed for survival, hiking, camping, and emergency signaling.
• Fullscreen Screen Flasher: Transmit optical signals in high-visibility amber or bright white directly from your display when a flashlight isn't available.
• Adjustable Transmission Speed: Fine-tune WPM (Words Per Minute) and Farnsworth timing for beginner clarity or high-speed communication.

📻 TACTILE TELEGRAPH KEY & AUDIO SYNTHESIZER
• Real Straight Key: Experience manual telegraphy with a responsive tapping key (short tap for dot ·, hold for dash —).
• Realistic Audio Tones: Crystal-clear audio frequency synthesis with customizable pitch (400 Hz to 1000 Hz).
• Haptic Vibration Feedback: Feel every dot and dash with realistic physical haptic vibration responses.
• Soundpacks: Switch between classic CW sidetone, vintage radio clicks, and tactical beeps.

🎓 190+ INTERACTIVE CURRICULUM LEVELS
• Progressive Training: Master the international Morse alphabet step-by-step using proven auditory recognition methods.
• Listen & Identify Mode: Hear the signal, watch the rhythm, and identify letters on an interactive tactical keyboard.
• Transmission Mode: Practice your keying rhythm by tapping target letters and words with precision timing feedback.
• Prosigns & Q-Codes: Advance beyond the basics to master procedural signals (AR, AS, SK, BK) and radio Q-codes (QTH, QSL, QRM).
• Instant Feedback: Immediate audio-visual guidance with animated speaker ripples, error indicators, and rhythm hints.

📖 COMPLETE MORSE CODE DICTIONARY
• Quick lookup dictionary for letters (A–Z), digits (0–9), punctuation, and emergency prosigns.
• One-tap preview to hear and flash any character instantly.

🔥 DAILY CODEBREAKER & GAMIFICATION
• Daily Secret Transmission: Crack a new encrypted daily Morse word to earn bonus XP and maintain your daily streak.
• Streaks & XP: Track your learning mastery and unlock tactical themes (Amber CRT, Phosphor Green, Cyber Cyan, Solar Gold).

🌲 100% OFFLINE & SURVIVAL READY
• No Internet Required: All transmitter tools, dictionary entries, and training levels function completely offline without cell signal.
• Zero Account Hassle: No registration, no login, and no personal data collection.
