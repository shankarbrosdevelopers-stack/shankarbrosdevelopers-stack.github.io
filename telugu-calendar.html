ABOUT THE APP

Telugu Calendar 2026 - పంచాంగం

While we strive for accuracy, users are encouraged to cross-verify important dates and timings with local Panchangam authorities or official sources. Experience a comprehensive and beautifully designed Telugu Calendar 2026, thoughtfully created for users in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. This app is designed to help you stay connected with traditional Telugu culture, Panchangam details, and devotional content in a simple and modern format.

Stay rooted in tradition with this all-in-one Telugu Panchangam app. Built with a clean and modern interface, it offers both Dark and Light modes for comfortable viewing anytime. Whether you're checking daily Tithi details or planning upcoming festivals, all essential information is easily accessible in one place.

🌙 Core Features

2026 Monthly Calendar View
View a clear monthly calendar highlighting Pournami, Amavasya, and major Telugu festivals.

Detailed Panchangam Information
Access daily Panchangam details such as Tithi, Nakshatram, Yogam, and Karanam, presented for easy understanding.

Daily Astrology
Read Rasi Phalalu for all 12 zodiac signs, along with daily and yearly astrology insights provided for reference purposes.

Devotional Collection
Explore a curated collection of Vedic slokas and stotrams with complete Telugu lyrics for daily spiritual practice.

Spiritual Wisdom
Read selected content from Hindu epics and Puranas, presented for educational and devotional interest.

🎨 Designed for Ease of Use

Modern Interface
Smooth, fast, and intuitive design inspired by Material 3 guidelines.

Offline Access
Access the calendar and core Panchangam details even without an internet connection.

Festival Alerts
Get reminders for important Telugu festivals (Pandugalu) and auspicious timings like Amrutha Kalam.

Location-Based Calculations
Sunrise, sunset, Rahu Kalam, and other timings are calculated based on regional location references.
