ABOUT THE APP

Vastu Compass: Home & Office

Unlock Harmony and Prosperity with the Ultimate Spatial Wisdom Guide

Transform your living and working spaces into sources of positive energy with a comprehensive guide to ancient architectural principles. Whether you are moving into a new home, renovating an office, or optimizing your current layout, this tool provides the directional insights and traditional knowledge you need.

Key Features:

🧭 Precise Directional Compass
Go beyond basic navigation. Our accurate digital tool provides real-time mapping of traditional zones, including corresponding Deities and natural elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space) for every corner of your property.

🤖 Multilingual AI Assistant
Have a specific question about your layout? Chat with our intelligent assistant. Get instant, expert advice on room placements, remedies, and spatial adjustments—available in 12+ Indian languages including Hindi, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, and more.

🏠 Room-by-Room Guidelines
Detailed tips and "Sacred Areas" mapping for every part of your home:

Entrance: Welcome positive energy through the right threshold.
Kitchen: Align the fire element for health and nutrition.
Master Bedroom: Enhance stability and peace for better rest.
Prayer Room: Create a sacred center for spiritual growth.
Wealth & Treasury: Positioning tips to attract prosperity and protect assets.
🌿 Modern Living Solutions
Practical advice for modern apartments and offices, covering Color Psychology, mirror placements, and the importance of the Brahmasthan (the central energy core).

🌐 12+ Languages Supported
Access traditional wisdom in your mother tongue. We support: English, हिन्दी (Hindi), తెలుగు (Telugu), বাংলা (Bengali), मराठी (Marathi), தமிழ் (Tamil), ગુજરાતી (Gujarati), اردو (Urdu), ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada), ଓଡ଼ିଆ (Odia), മലയാളം (Malayalam), and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi).

✅ Ad-Free Experience
Enjoy a focused and peaceful study of your space. Watch a simple rewarded video to remove interruptions for up to 7 days, allowing you to optimize your environment in peace.

The science of Vastu Shastra is about balancing the five elements of nature to bring health, wealth, and happiness. Our app combines this Vedic heritage with modern AI technology to give you a personalized directional guide in the palm of your hand.
