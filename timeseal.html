ABOUT THE APP

TimeSeal: GPS Timestamp Camera

TimeSeal is the heavy-duty GPS timestamp camera engineered for civil engineering, construction verification, utility audits, land surveying, field inspections, and legal documentation.

Capture tamper-evident photos and HD video with live GPS coordinates, reverse-geocoded addresses, compass azimuth heading, altitude, and project notes embedded permanently onto every frame.

FIELD-READY FEATURE SUITE

HIGH-PRECISION GPS & COORDINATES
• Dual Coordinate Systems: Decimal Degrees (DD) and Degrees Minutes Seconds (DMS)
• Live Fix Accuracy: View satellite fix tolerance (±m/ft) directly on the stamp
• Elevation Tracking: Altitude measurement relative to sea level
• Automated Reverse Geocoding: Auto-stamp street address, city, state, and country
• Embedded GPS Mini-Map: Aerial mini-map watermark with directional heading beam

MILITARY-GRADE TIMESTAMPING
• Millisecond Precision: Evidentiary-grade time synchronization with millisecond toggle
• Global Date Formats: ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD), European (DD/MM/YYYY), and US (MM/DD/YYYY)
• 12-Hour & 24-Hour Clock: Toggle standard AM/PM and 24-hour military time
• Sequential Indexing: Optional sequential shot numbering (#0001) for chain of custody

SENSORS & LEVELING HUD
• 360° Magnetic Compass: Live azimuth heading with cardinal points
• Pitch & Roll Indicators: Digital leveling sensors ensure calibrated captures
• Industrial UI: High-contrast gunmetal and safety yellow design tuned for bright sunlight

MULTI-LANGUAGE & GLOBAL READY
• Native Localization: Full support for international crews and multi-regional teams
• In-App Language Switch: Easily toggle English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Indonesian, Vietnamese, and more
• Fast Onboarding: Select language on first launch or switch anytime in settings

PROJECT PROFILES & BRANDING
• Project Templates: Quick-switch between Civil, Survey, Logistics, or custom profiles
• Custom Metadata: Overlay Project Title, Contractor Name, Inspector ID, and Notes
• Custom Company Logo: Import and stamp your company logo directly onto the card
• Card Placement: Dock watermark at Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, or Bottom-Right
• Styling Controls: Customize font size, card opacity, and border styling

DUAL CAPTURE (PHOTO & VIDEO)
• High-Resolution Photos: High-fidelity sensor output with zero compression loss
• Live Stamped Video: Record HD video with real-time updating timestamp, coordinates, and HUD
• Hardware EXIF Writer: Embeds GPS, altitude, capture datetime, device model, and tags into JPEG EXIF headers for desktop GIS, AutoCAD, and Google Earth integration
• Quick-Access Preview: In-app thumbnail for instant verification of captured media

100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE
• Zero Cloud Transmission: Photos, videos, coordinates, and notes stay locally on your device
• Fully Offline Ready: Works reliably in remote job sites without cell service
• Ad-Free Pro Option: One-time upgrade to remove ads for uninterrupted fieldwork
