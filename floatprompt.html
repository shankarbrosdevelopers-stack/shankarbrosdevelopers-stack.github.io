ABOUT THE APP

FloatPrompt: Teleprompter

Read your scripts with ease while maintaining perfect eye contact with the camera.

Whether you are recording a vlog, presenting in a virtual meeting, or using a physical teleprompter glass rig, this app provides a clean and highly customizable script reading experience. Switch seamlessly between a classic full-screen display and a convenient floating widget overlay.

🌐 GLOBAL LANGUAGE SUPPORT
Speak and read in your native language! The app interface is fully localized and supports 12 major languages:

English, Español, Français, Deutsch, हिन्दी (Hindi), 中文 (Chinese), 日本語 (Japanese), العربية (Arabic), Русский (Russian), 한국어 (Korean), Bahasa Indonesia, and Português.
🌟 KEY FEATURES
1. Floating Widget Overlay
Multitask Seamlessly: Read your script while using your device's native camera app, social media recording tools, or video meeting platforms (such as Zoom, Teams, or Meet).
Adjustable Transparency: Control the opacity of the widget background so it doesn't block your view of other apps.
Fully Resizable & Draggable: Place the widget anywhere on your screen, keeping it close to your camera lens to maintain natural eye contact.
2. Classic Full-Screen Mode
Distraction-Free Reading: A dedicated full-screen layout designed for rehearsals, public speaking, presentations, and voiceovers.
Mirror / Reverse Text Support: Reverse the text horizontally to use the app with physical teleprompter glass rigs and beam splitter monitors.
3. Complete Text Customization
Adjustable Scroll Speed: Fine-tune the scrolling speed to match your natural speaking pace.
Font & Size Controls: Increase or decrease text size for comfortable reading at any distance.
Themes & Colors: Change the text color and background contrast to suit your lighting environment, including support for dark and light modes.
4. Easy Script Management
Quick Creation & Editing: Write, edit, and organize your scripts directly within the app.
Local Storage: Your scripts are stored securely on your device, ensuring privacy and offline accessibility.
🎬 WHO IS IT FOR?
Content Creators & Vloggers: Record high-quality videos for YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram without memorizing lines.
Business Professionals: Deliver polished presentations and participate in video calls without looking away from the screen.
Public Speakers: Rehearse speeches and adjust formatting to ensure a smooth, natural delivery.
Podcasters & Voice Artists: Read voiceover scripts without paper rustling sounds.
🔒 PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS
Draw Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): This permission is strictly used to display the floating teleprompter widget over other apps.
Your scripts are kept locally on your device and are never shared or uploaded to external servers.
