ABOUT THE APP

Ruler: Tape Measure & Scale

Turn your phone into an ultra-precise digital ruler, caliper, and drafting measurement toolkit!

Whether you need to measure small items in centimeters, millimeters, or fractional inches, align surfaces with a 2D spirit bubble level, or inspect angles with a 360° protractor, Ruler delivers laser-accurate results directly on your screen.

Designed with modern engineering aesthetics and smooth haptic feedback, this is the ultimate pocket measurement tool for carpenters, DIYers, engineers, crafters, students, and designers.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📐 ALL-IN-ONE MEASUREMENT TOOLS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• DIGITAL SCREEN RULER: Measure length and width with ease. Supports Millimeters (mm), Centimeters (cm), Decimal Inches, and Fractional Inches (e.g., 3 5/16").
• DUAL CALIPER SLIDERS: Smooth two-finger touch calipers for measuring small parts, screws, bolts, coins, and jewelry with pinpoint accuracy.
• DUAL-EDGE SCALE: Display metric on one edge and imperial on the other simultaneously for instant unit comparison.
• 2D AREA CALCULATOR: Drag length and width calipers to calculate surface areas in real time.
• 360° PROTRACTOR: Measure internal and external angles, slopes, and bevels effortlessly.
• 2D SPIRIT BUBBLE LEVEL: High-precision surface leveler powered by device tilt sensors with real-time degrees readout.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🎯 SIMPLE & EXACT CALIBRATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every device screen is unique. Ruler ensures 100% true physical accuracy with two simple calibration methods:
1. Credit Card Calibration: Use any standard ISO credit or debit card for instant 1-tap calibration.
2. Physical Ruler: Align with any physical ruler or tape measure to fine-tune down to the exact sub-millimeter.
