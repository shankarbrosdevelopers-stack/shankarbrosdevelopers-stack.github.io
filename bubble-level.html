ABOUT THE APP

Bubble Level: Pro Spirit Level

Turn your phone into a high-precision spirit level! Traditional bubble levels and plumb bobs are essential for accuracy, but they are rarely there when you need them. TrueLevel: Pro Bubble Tool gives you professional-grade levelling right in your pocket.

Whether you are a professional contractor, a DIY enthusiast, or a photographer, TrueLevel provides the accuracy you need with a simple, intuitive interface.

🛠 Key Professional Features
Triple-Mode Levelling: Precision horizontal, vertical, and 360° surface levelling.

Sound Feedback: Get an audible reminder when you hit the perfect level—ideal for when the screen is out of sight.

Measurement Lock: Use the Screen Lock feature to keep your readings consistent for repetitive tasks.

One-Tap Calibration: Instantly reset and calibrate for pinpoint accuracy tailored to your specific device sensors.

Work Anywhere: Works woodworking level, or slope finder.

🏠 Real-World Applications
Home & Decor: Hang photo frames, shelves, and mirrors perfectly straight.

Construction & Carpentry: Calibrate tables, assemble cabinets, and build DIY projects with confidence.

Photography & Painting: Align your tripod for the perfect horizon or ensure your canvas is perfectly square.

Office & Indoors: Flatten desks, set up monitors, and organize your workspace with professional precision.
