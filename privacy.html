Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2026

At Shankarbros.dev, your privacy is our priority. We build applications with a strong commitment to digital minimalism, which means we only collect the absolute minimum information necessary to make our apps function.

1. Information We Collect

Our apps are designed to work locally on your device whenever possible. We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) unless you explicitly provide it to us (e.g., when contacting support).

  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking software in our apps.
  • Local Storage: Your data (such as app preferences or generated content) is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

Any information you choose to share with us via email or support channels is used solely for the purpose of resolving your inquiry or improving our services. We never sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

While our core applications do not rely on third parties, some apps may optionally integrate with external services (e.g., if you choose to share a file or link). In such cases, the privacy policies of those third-party services apply.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.
