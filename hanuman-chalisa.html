ABOUT THE APP

Hanuman Chalisa: Read & Listen

Experience divine peace and spiritual strength with a modern, thoughtfully crafted devotional companion. Designed for devotees worldwide, this app brings sacred hymns, interactive daily rituals, and guided recitation into your daily routine.

Whether you are chanting in the morning, listening during commutes, or completing a personal vow, every feature is designed to be calm, respectful, and distraction-free.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ KEY HIGHLIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🪔 Interactive Pocket Mandir
Begin your day with authentic temple rituals:
• Light the sacred Diya (automatically resets at midnight for your next morning prayer)
• Offer fresh flowers with a gentle touch
• Ring resonant temple brass bells and blow the sacred Shankh (conch)

📖 Multi-Language Recitation
Read verses with clear typography and verse-by-verse meanings across 11 languages:
• Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Odia, and Urdu.

📜 Hands-Free Auto-Scroll & Text-to-Speech
• Enable smooth auto-scroll to recite comfortably without touching the screen.
• Listen to pronunciation verse-by-verse with integrated speech assistance.

🎧 Synchronized Audio & Lyric Chanting
• High-quality studio recitations with karaoke-style synchronized lyrics.
• Flexible repeat counters (1x, 3x, 7x, 11x, 21x, 108x) for japa and continuous meditation.
• Background audio playback with convenient lock-screen media controls.

🚩 Sankalpa Challenge (21 & 40 Days)
• Dedicate your prayers with specific intentions (health, peace, courage, prosperity, removal of obstacles).
• Track your consistency and build a long-lasting spiritual habit.

🌙 Sacred Temple Dark Mode
• High-contrast warm amber and golden palette crafted specifically for comfortable reading before dawn and late at night.

📅 Practice & Streak Tracker
• Monitor your daily reading progress, listen counts, and habit streaks.
• Daily verse cards with one-tap sharing to send morning blessings to family and friends.

📚 Complete Spiritual Library
Explore more sacred texts within one app:
• Bajrang Baan
• Sankat Mochan Hanuman Ashtak
• Aarti & Sundarkand selections
• Spiritual significance, benefits, and mythological katha
